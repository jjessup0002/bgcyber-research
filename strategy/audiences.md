# Audiences

## Local consumer audience

Residents and nearby visitors who need a low-effort answer to “what is worth doing, trying, or knowing around Bowling Green?” The early Facebook evidence suggests family planners, food/discovery seekers, local-event participants, and people responding to civic/sports/local-pride moments. These are behavior segments, not fixed demographic claims.

## Merchant/business audience

Local owners and operators who need affordable, credible awareness and reusable social assets. Their desired outcome is not merely a listing: it is an attractive introduction, a reason to reshare, and evidence that the feature can reach relevant local people.

## Implication

One post may serve both audiences only when consumer usefulness survives. Merchant-facing acquisition, onboarding, LinkedIn activity, and paid opportunities should not overwhelm the consumer feed.
