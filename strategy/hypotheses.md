# Hypothesis Register

## H-001 — Discovery first

- **Hypothesis:** useful local discovery/story content outperforms explicit standalone coupon advertising for earned attention.
- **Why it matters:** determines the page's consumer value and promotional mix.
- **Evidence for:** planning/event and personality examples in `research/bowling-green-market.md`.
- **Evidence against:** no internal comparison; offers may work for high-intent audiences.
- **Confidence:** moderate.
- **Prediction:** matched discovery features earn more shares, qualitative comments, profile visits, and earned follows than deal graphics.
- **Experiment:** five matched merchant feature/deal pairs.
- **Status:** researching. **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-10.

## H-002 — People beat generic promotion

- **Hypothesis:** owner/staff/person-led original content outperforms generic branded graphics for local businesses.
- **Evidence for:** Morris Jewelry and Bowling Green Athletic Club within-page comparisons.
- **Evidence against:** category, novelty, and production quality confound the comparison.
- **Confidence:** moderate. **Prediction:** a people-led version wins more shares/comments than matched graphic version.
- **Experiment:** paired pilot. **Status:** supported externally; untested internally. **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-10.

## H-003 — Merchant reshares distribute early

- **Hypothesis:** featured merchants can supply meaningful early distribution through reshares.
- **Evidence for:** local pages repeatedly cross-promote events/businesses; no BGCD proof.
- **Evidence against:** merchant participation may be passive or audiences may overlap.
- **Confidence:** low. **Prediction:** at least three of five featured merchants reshare within 72 hours and referral signals appear.
- **Experiment:** instrument five feature releases. **Status:** proposed. **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-10.

## H-004 — Platforms have different jobs

- **Hypothesis:** Facebook should prioritize local planning/conversation/shareability, Instagram visual discovery/reels, and LinkedIn merchant credibility/business development.
- **Evidence for:** platform behavior is directionally different; no BGCD tests.
- **Evidence against:** operational capacity may make separate execution impractical.
- **Confidence:** moderate. **Prediction:** platform-native variants outperform identical cross-posts on their target metric.
- **Experiment:** three-topic native-format pilot. **Status:** researching. **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-10.
