# Positioning

## Working position — experimental

**People follow BG Cyber Deals because it helps them find worthwhile things to try, do, and know in Bowling Green before they miss them.**

Consumer value: timely, specific local discovery that lowers the friction of deciding where to go, what to try, and what is newly worth attention. Merchant value: credible introduction to local residents through useful editorial storytelling and transparent promotion options.

This position is not “the page for every ad,” a generic coupon stream, an unverified news source, or a restaurant-only account. It must be validated by measured content performance, merchant reshares, follows, and downstream business interest.

## Differentiation guardrails

- Consumer usefulness precedes merchant promotion.
- Feature real people, proof, place, and specificity—not interchangeable templates.
- Label sponsored inclusion and preserve editorial selection standards.
- Avoid sensitive public-safety rumor aggregation and claims not independently supported.
