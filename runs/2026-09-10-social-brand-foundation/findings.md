# Findings

## Evidence — moderate, incomplete

Event planning and seasonal local experience material generated the clearest sharing signals, especially Jackson's Orchard Apple Fest posts and City Parks event roundups. The common element is not merely “events”: these posts answer an imminent decision with tangible details. Within two unrelated local business pages, staff/coworker humor outperformed adjacent generic product/program material. Routine notices and generic templates were generally weak.

## Interpretation

BG Cyber Deals has reason to test a locally specific discovery model that combines useful planning information, real people, and a clear reason to care now. The sample does not establish a universal content formula or prove commercial conversion.

## Contradictions

Strong attention is sometimes tied to sensitive civic/news content, which is not a fitting commercial brand lane. Seasonal success may not be portable to off-season. Some recommendation conversations are valuable despite low reaction totals.

## Recommended experiments

Run E-001 and E-002 in `content/experiments.md`; establish a metric baseline before claiming a strategy winner.
