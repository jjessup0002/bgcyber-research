# Plan

## Question

What local Facebook attention patterns should inform the first BG Cyber Deals social/brand hypotheses, and what must be tested internally rather than assumed?

## Prior knowledge and gaps

The inherited chat research contains a 141-item Facebook observation archive. It is strong enough to seed hypotheses but incomplete on source breadth, business-page breadth, platform comparisons, and internal outcomes.

## Useful evidence criteria

Recent public posts with durable reference, visible engagement where available, within-account comparisons, repeated cross-source patterns, and comment evidence where accessible. Avoid treating personalized feeds or public-safety spikes as commercial demand proof.

## Stopping criteria

Complete the durable system, ingest the known archive without fabrication, extract bounded findings, and shift high-decision uncertainty to experiments rather than continuing an unbounded Facebook sweep.
