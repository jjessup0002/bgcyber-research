# Sources

The source register contains 29 reviewed Facebook sources/communities; the raw 141-row evidence archive is `research/facebook-attention-observations.csv`. High-value reviewed sources include public community groups (HIP Bowling Green, Bowling Green Connect, Bowling Green Life); municipal/tourism/event pages; local sports; local attractions; food/family destinations; retail and fitness businesses; and local news as ecosystem context.

Dates and canonical URLs were retained when captured. Where prior collection recorded only a page reference, the absence of a post permalink is explicitly preserved rather than reconstructed.
