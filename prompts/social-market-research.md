# Social Market Research Program

## Central question

What should BG Cyber Deals become on social media so Bowling Green residents follow voluntarily even when not seeking a coupon, while merchants see features as worthwhile exposure?

## Procedure

1. Read the repository orientation and current state; turn the decision question into specific evidence gaps.
2. Check registers for prior coverage and define a nonduplicate sample across local discovery media, city lifestyle accounts, food/activity accounts, small-business creators, destination/chamber/downtown organizations, event/community publications, and deal platforms. Include strong and weak examples where useful.
3. Separate Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and short-form video research. For each record: hook, subject, series, human presence, visual treatment, caption/CTA, merchant tags, collaboration/reshares, promotion treatment, cadence, and visible engagement.
4. Assess local transferability: market size, audience role, production requirement, merchant availability, and local cultural fit. Do not import a big-city tactic by default.
5. Save each item to registers; update synthesis and hypotheses only when evidence warrants it.
6. Stop when the question has enough independent evidence, new sources are redundant, internal experimentation is the better next step, or an access barrier makes further work unproductive.

## Evidence requirements

Record URL/reference and dates. Use blank metrics when unavailable. Distinguish observed behavior, interpretation, and recommendation. Seek comments/tags/reshares/saves where visible; do not infer conversion or follower growth from raw popularity.

## Output

Create a dated run folder with plan, sources, findings, and retrospective. Surface contradictions and recommend an experiment when uncertainty remains decision-critical.
