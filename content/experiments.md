# Experiments

## E-001 — Story feature versus standalone deal

Publish five matched merchant pairs: one original person/place-led discovery feature and one conventional offer-led graphic. Keep subject, release window, merchant support, and CTA as comparable as practical. Track shares, saves, comment quality, profile visits, earned followers, merchant reshares, page/business clicks, and merchant inquiry—not likes alone. Status: proposed.

## E-002 — Merchant reshare loop

Ask five featured merchants to reshare a prepared asset within 72 hours. Record whether they did, audience response, tracked referral signals if possible, and what made the asset easy/hard to share. Status: proposed.
