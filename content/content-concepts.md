# Content Concepts

- “Three things you need to know before taking the kids to ___ this weekend.”
- “We asked the owner what first-timers miss at ___.”
- “A Bowling Green plan under $20: here is exactly what that gets you.”
- “The local thing people keep tagging friends in—here is the practical version.”
- “Not a deal alert: why this small local place is on our radar.”
- “One question BG keeps asking, checked with the people who actually know.”

All concepts require current factual verification and a matched control when used as an experiment.
