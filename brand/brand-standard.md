# Brand Standard — Initial, Evidence-Informed

BG Cyber Deals should operate as a dependable local discovery companion: specific, curious, practical, and warm. The feed earns the right to include merchant opportunities by helping a resident make a better local decision first.

**Consumer promise:** “Worth knowing before your next Bowling Green plan.”  
**Merchant promise:** “We help the right local people understand what makes your business worth trying.”

Not the brand: an unverified news page, an all-purpose directory, an ad dump, a national-template account, or a page that implies every included business is independently endorsed.

Every post should pass: Is it locally specific? Is there a clear reader payoff? Is the sponsorship/editorial status plain? Would this still be useful if the business did not pay?
