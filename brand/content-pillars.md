# Content Pillars — Testable Initial Set

1. **Before You Make Plans** — practical, current answers to what is happening, open, new, or easy to do. Formats: weekly carousel/reel, map-like guide. Risk: stale information.
2. **Try This, BG** — a specific food, product, activity, or experience with proof and context. Formats: short reel/photo feature. Risk: becoming pay-to-play.
3. **The People Who Make It Local** — owner/staff stories and unusual origin/skill details. Formats: 30–60 second interview/reel. Risk: generic founder bio.
4. **Worth the Group Chat** — participation prompts, local rituals, seasonal moments, and constrained comparisons. Formats: shareable question/carousel. Risk: low-value engagement bait.
5. **Local Knowledge** — clear answers drawn from repeated public questions: price, timing, parking, age fit, availability, what to expect. Formats: explainer/card. Risk: accuracy/maintenance burden.

Pillar selection is provisional. Test shares, comments, earned follows, merchant reshares, and downstream interest—not likes alone.
