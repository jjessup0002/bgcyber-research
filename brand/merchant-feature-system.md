# Merchant Feature System

## Intake

Ask for: owner/staff names and permission; origin story; best first thing to try; unexpected detail; current offer if any; customer/problem served; photos/video; location/hours; social handles; permission for tagging/collaboration/reshares; factual-review contact.

## Produce

Create one consumer-useful feature, a platform-native short cut, a merchant reshare asset, and an optional clearly labeled offer. Editorial selection and sponsored inclusion must be visibly distinguishable.

## Quality check

Verify facts, show a real local proof point, name why a resident should care, avoid unverifiable claims, disclose payment, and measure merchant reshare plus audience response.
