# Platform Strategy

**Facebook:** planning utility, shareable local moments, event/season updates, conversation prompts, and merchant/community reshares. Favor posts that lower decision friction.

**Instagram:** visually rich discovery, short owner/staff reels, place/product proof, saves, and collaborations. Do not simply resize Facebook copy.

**LinkedIn:** merchant-facing credibility: introductions, business stories, local ecosystem insights, partnership and content-production proof. Do not duplicate consumer deal posts.

Treat these as working jobs. Validate with native-format experiments and production-capacity review.
