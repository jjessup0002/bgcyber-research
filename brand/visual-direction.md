# Visual Direction

Prefer original local photos and short video that show the person, place, product-in-use, or experience. Use natural proof: owner/staff faces, hands making something, an identifiable interior/exterior, menu/item detail, and actual visitor context when permission allows. Keep thumbnail overlays brief and local-specific.

Templates may organize information, but should not replace the proof. Use stock imagery only for genuinely abstract information; do not use AI imagery to imply a real local business/experience. Keep logo use restrained. A feature should visually feel like it could only have been made in Bowling Green.
