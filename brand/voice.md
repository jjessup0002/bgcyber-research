# Voice

Use direct, observant, lightly playful sentences. Lead with the local detail that changes a plan: what it is, where, why now, and who it is for. Write like a plugged-in neighbor with standards, not an agency brochure.

**Use:** “This is the kind of fall plan you send to the family group chat.”  
**Avoid:** “Unlock an unforgettable experience with this premier local destination.”

Be warm but not hypey. Use humor when it arises from a real local detail, never at a resident's expense. Keep calls to action natural: “Save this for Saturday,” “Tag the person who always picks dessert,” or “What should we investigate next?” Avoid hard-sell phrases, fake urgency, empty superlatives, and generic AI/agency language.
