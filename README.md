# BG Cyber Deals Research System

This repository is the durable evidence, decision, and learning system for BG Cyber Deals. It is not a content dump or a substitute for ClickUp. GitHub stores the research record; ClickUp stores the small set of actions warranted by research.

## Current orientation

The first program is local brand, social, discovery, audience-growth, and merchant-marketing research for Bowling Green, Kentucky. The leading working thesis is that the company may earn more durable attention as a useful local discovery/media brand than as a coupon feed. It remains a hypothesis, not a settled business decision.

Start with [research-state.md](research-state.md), then [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md). Evidence registers are under `research/`; active strategic thinking is under `strategy/`; reusable content operations are under `brand/` and `content/`; dated work is under `runs/`.

## Evidence standard

- **Observation**: what a source visibly showed, with date and URL/reference.
- **Interpretation**: a bounded explanation of one or more observations.
- **Hypothesis**: a testable claim that has not been proven by internal performance.
- **Decision**: a documented choice, with status and supersession history.

Public engagement metrics are recorded only when visible. Blank fields mean unavailable, never estimated. Current Facebook evidence is a directional sample, not a population study or proof of purchase intent.

## System map

| Location | Role |

## Working rules

1. Do not treat three examples as a general rule.
2. Preserve contradictions and older decisions rather than overwriting them.
3. Check registers before opening a source again; revisit only for a defined reason.
4. Create ClickUp work only when a concrete, research-backed action exists.
5. Let internal BG Cyber Deals performance gradually outweigh external advice.
