# Competitors and Analogues

This register intentionally begins as a research gap rather than pretending that local pages are direct competitors. Next analogue work should sample local discovery media, city lifestyle accounts, food/activity creators, destination organizations, community publications, and coupon/deal pages in comparable small-to-mid-size markets.

For every analogue, record its platform job, audience, editorial/promotion mix, recurring formats, merchant-tagging behavior, evidence of reshares/saves/comments, monetization treatment, and transferability to Bowling Green. Do not import a Nashville/Austin tactic without a local-size and production-capacity assessment.
