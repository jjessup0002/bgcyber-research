# Competitors and Analogues

## Initial analogue evidence

| Analogue | Observed model | Transferable principle | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Everyday BG](https://everydaybg.com/) | Local guide across food, activities, resources, calendar, deals, and multiple social platforms; founder-led identity; sponsor references visible. | A Bowling Green discovery brand can organize specific, repeatable guide categories around an identifiable local point of view. | Website inventory does not reveal social engagement, economics, or consumer trust. Treat it as a direct market-adjacent model to study, not proof of performance. |
| [Nashville Guru](https://nashvilleguru.com/welcome) | Discovery utility organized by neighborhoods, events, happy hours, eating/drinking, music, sports, kids, shop; combines newsletter, discounts, partnerships/advertising. | Separate evergreen navigation/utility from timely editorial and disclose commercial participation. | Nashville's scale, resources, and visitor economy are materially different. |
| [Louisville Family Fun](https://louisvillefamilyfun.net/weekend-events) | Recurring weekly, date-ordered family-event choices with explicit context and newsletter capture. | A narrowly defined recurring planning franchise can become habitual when it filters many options into a usable weekly selection. | It serves a parent/family niche and is not a general-city model. |
| [BG Area Chamber social guide](https://www.bgchamber.com/clientuploads/Social_Media_Guide_2026.pdf) | Requests business handles, reposts, tags, and Instagram collaboration acceptance for event promotion. | Merchant reshare/collaboration should be operationalized, requested before publishing, and tracked—not assumed. | It is an event/partner playbook, not proof that reshares grow a consumer discovery page. |

## Current assessment

The strongest transferable structure is **serviceable local utility plus a recognizable editorial point of view**, not “post local deals more often.” Everyday BG demonstrates that a broad, local discovery footprint already exists in Bowling Green, which makes differentiation and honest competitive research essential. Louisville Family Fun is a useful model for a bounded weekly planning franchise. Nashville Guru shows a more mature separation of categories, email, partnership, discounts, and advertising; those are architecture signals, not a market-size template.

Next, collect social-native post evidence from the analogue accounts: format, cadence, engagement, collaboration behavior, disclosure, and audience response. Do not import a Nashville/Austin tactic without local-size and production-capacity assessment.
