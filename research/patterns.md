# Patterns and Counterevidence

## Candidate patterns

1. **Plan-making utility spreads.** Event calendars, hours, availability, and “everything you need to know” posts showed high sharing in several sources.
2. **Local ritual + sensory specificity attracts attention.** Apple Fest treats, food, and seasonal family experience content were the strongest observed local examples.
3. **Human personality can outperform product-first posts.** Two independent local business pages showed this within-page pattern.
4. **Low reactions do not equal no value.** Recommendation questions can contain decision-stage comments with small public reaction totals.

## Counterevidence and limits

- A high share count may reflect the unique event, source audience, or timing rather than the content format.
- Much of the strongest evidence is seasonal and may not transfer outside fall/event periods.
- Community/news attention is not necessarily suitable commercial attention.
- No current internal BG Cyber Deals results confirm any external pattern.
